#### Phase 3: Authority & Scale (Month 4+)
1. **Case Studies** - Published case studies on website & platforms
2. **Testimonials** - Collect and display client testimonials
3. **SEO Content** - Blog posts targeting service keywords

#### Phase 4: Premium Positioning (Month 8+)
1. **Retainer Model** - Transition high-value clients to retainers (£500-£2,000/month)
2. **Premium Services** - Package bundled services at higher margins
3. **White-Label Partnerships** - Become white-label dev partner for other agencies

## 9. TAX & COMPLIANCE

### 9.1 Corporation Tax Obligations (UK)

**Filing Requirements:**
- **CT600 Deadline:** 12 months after accounting period end
- **Tax Payment Deadline:** 9 months + 1 day after period end
- **Statutory Accounts:** Required for all limited companies
- **Registration with HMRC:** Must complete within 3 months of trading

**Tax Rates (2025-26):**
- Profits £0-£50,000: **19%**
- Profits £50,001-£250,000: Sliding scale (19%-25%)
- Profits >£250,000: **25%**

**Example Year 2 Tax Calculation:**
- Operating Profit: £31,000
- Corporation Tax @ 19%: £5,890
- Net Profit after Tax: £25,110

### 9.2 VAT Considerations

**Threshold (2025):** £85,000 annual turnover

**Action Points:**
- If turnover projected >£85,000 (likely by Year 1 end): Register for VAT
- Benefits: Reclaim input VAT on expenses
- Disadvantage: Add 20% to client invoices (may lose price competitiveness)

**Recommendation:** Register voluntarily if clients are VATable businesses (B2B) as you can recover VAT on costs

### 9.3 PAYE (Payroll Tax)

**Obligations with Employees:**
- **Monthly Reporting:** Real-time information (RTI) to HMRC
- **Employer NI:** ~15% on earnings above £9,100/year per employee
- **Payroll Software:** Xero/FreshBooks handles compliance
- **Auto-Enrolment Pension:** Required for all employees (5% contribution)

**Year 1 Payroll Tax Estimate:**
- Gross Salaries: £180,000
- Employer NI (15%): £16,764
- Pension Contributions (5%): £9,000
- **Total PAYE Cost:** £25,764

### 9.4 Dividends & Personal Tax

**Dividend Taxation:**
- Only pay dividends from retained profits
- Dividend allowance: £500/year (tax-free)
- Above £500: 8.75% (basic rate), 33.75% (higher rate)

**Recommended Strategy:**
- Retain profits in company for Year 1
- Pay dividends in Year 2 once profitable
- Combine salary (£12,570 personal allowance) + dividends for tax efficiency
